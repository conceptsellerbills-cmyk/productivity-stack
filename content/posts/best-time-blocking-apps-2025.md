## Why Time Blocking Actually Works

Time blocking is the practice of scheduling every hour of your workday — assigning specific tasks to specific time slots rather than working from a vague to-do list. Elon Musk, Bill Gates, and Cal Newport all swear by it.

The research backs it up: context switching costs knowledge workers an average of 23 minutes of recovery time per interruption. When you block time for focused work, you eliminate the switching and reclaim those lost hours.

The challenge is doing it consistently. That's where time blocking apps come in.

## What to Look for in a Time Blocking App

- **Calendar integration**: Syncs with Google Calendar and/or Outlook
- **Task management**: Can pull tasks from your to-do list automatically
- **AI scheduling**: Smart enough to suggest optimal times for different work types
- **Time tracking**: Shows where your time actually went vs. where you planned
- **Buffer time**: Automatically protects time between meetings

## Reclaim.ai — Best for AI-Powered Scheduling

Reclaim.ai connects to your Google Calendar and automatically finds open time slots for your tasks, habits, and meeting buffers.

**How it works**: You tell Reclaim your tasks with estimated durations and deadlines. Reclaim schedules them into your calendar automatically, works around your meetings, and reschedules when things change.

**Standout features:**
- Smart 1:1 meeting scheduling with team members
- Habit scheduling (block gym time, lunch, focused work)
- Buffer time protection around meetings
- Automatic rescheduling when conflicts arise
- Slack and Asana integration

**Pricing**: Free tier available. Starter at $8/month, Business at $12/month/seat.

**Best for**: Knowledge workers with unpredictable meeting schedules who want AI to handle the scheduling puzzle.

Sunsama takes a different approach: daily planning as a mindful ritual. Each morning, you pull in tasks from connected tools (Asana, Todoist, Linear, Notion, GitHub) and build your day's time blocks in one guided session.

**Standout features:**
- Pulls tasks from 10+ tools into one planning view

- Beautiful, focused interface

**Pricing**: $16/month (annual plan). No free tier — 14-day trial.

**Best for**: Professionals who want a structured daily planning practice and use multiple task management tools.

## Todoist + Google Calendar — Best Free Combo

If you're budget-conscious, combine Todoist (free tier) with Google Calendar's built-in time blocking:

1. List your tasks in Todoist with priorities
2. Each morning, open Google Calendar
3. Create events for each task block
4. Use the "Tasks" sidebar in Google Calendar to drag tasks onto your calendar

No app cost, integrates with everything, and the friction of manual scheduling forces you to be intentional.

## Building Your Time Blocking System

Regardless of tool, effective time blocking follows these principles:

**Theme your days**: Mondays for planning and admin, Tuesdays/Thursdays for deep work, Wednesdays for meetings, Fridays for review and email.

**Protect morning focus time**: Block 2-3 hours each morning before checking email or Slack. This is when your most important work happens.

**Add buffer time**: Never book tasks back-to-back. 10-15 minute buffers prevent cascade failures.

**Review weekly**: Every Friday, review what you planned vs. what actually happened. Adjust your system accordingly.

The best time blocking app is the one you'll actually use every day. Start with your current calendar, add blocks manually, and upgrade to AI scheduling when you're consistent with the habit.
