# Best Goal Setting Apps in 2025: Top Tools to Achieve More

Setting goals is easy. Achieving them is hard — unless you have the right system. The best goal setting apps combine structure, accountability, and progress tracking to help you move from intention to action. Monthly search volume: ~28,000/month.

## Why Use a Goal Setting App?

Research consistently shows that written goals with clear metrics are 42% more likely to be achieved. Apps add reminders, progress visuals, and check-in habits that dramatically improve follow-through.

---

## Top 7 Goal Setting Apps for 2025

### 1. Notion — Most Flexible Goal System

**Best for:** Custom goal frameworks, teams, and knowledge workers

Notion isn't a dedicated goal app, but its flexibility makes it one of the most powerful options. Build OKR dashboards, weekly review templates, and habit trackers all in one workspace.

**Strengths:**
- Fully customizable goal templates
- Linked databases (goals → tasks → notes)
- Team-friendly with collaborative pages
- Free tier covers personal use

**Limitations:**
- Requires setup time — not plug-and-play
- No built-in reminders or notifications
- Can become overwhelming without structure

**Pricing:** Free plan; Plus $10/month; Teams $15/user/month

---

### 2. Weekdone — Best for OKR Goal Setting

**Best for:** Teams using the OKR (Objectives and Key Results) framework

Weekdone is built specifically around OKRs — the goal framework used by Google, Intel, and thousands of high-growth companies. It connects team-level OKRs to individual weekly plans.

**Strengths:**
- Purpose-built OKR tracking
- Weekly check-ins with progress scoring
- Visual dashboards for team visibility
- Integrates with Slack, Jira, and Google Workspace

**Limitations:**
- Expensive for larger teams
- Overkill for personal goal setting
- UI can feel dated compared to newer tools

**Pricing:** Free for up to 3 users; paid plans start at $108/quarter

---

### 3. Strides — Best iPhone Goal Tracker

**Best for:** iOS users who want a beautiful, structured goal app

Strides is one of the highest-rated goal tracking apps on iOS. It supports four goal types: habit goals, average goals, milestone goals, and project goals — giving you flexibility for any objective.

**Strengths:**
- Four distinct goal types
- Clean streak tracking with motivating visuals
- Customizable reminders
- Excellent iOS widget support

**Limitations:**
- iOS/Mac only — no Android
- Free plan limited to 3 goals
- No team or collaboration features

**Pricing:** Free (3 goals); Premium $4.99/month or $29.99/year

---

### 4. ClickUp Goals — Best for Task-Linked Goals

**Best for:** Teams wanting to connect high-level goals to daily tasks

ClickUp Goals lets you define targets (numeric, true/false, currency, task completion) and link them directly to tasks and projects. Progress updates automatically as work gets done.

**Strengths:**
- Targets auto-update from task completion
- Visual goal progress rollup
- Hierarchy: Portfolios → Goals → Targets
- Included in a full project management suite

**Limitations:**
- Feature-heavy — steep learning curve
- UI can feel cluttered
- Best value only if you already use ClickUp for tasks

**Pricing:** Free plan available; Unlimited $7/user/month

---

### 5. Goalify — Best Minimalist Goal App

**Best for:** People who want simple, distraction-free goal tracking

Goalify strips away complexity to focus purely on goals, daily check-ins, and progress streaks. If you've been overwhelmed by complex systems, Goalify is a refreshing alternative.

**Strengths:**
- Simple, clean interface
- Daily check-in reminders
- Visual streak tracking
- Available on iOS and Android

**Limitations:**
- Limited features vs. full-stack tools
- No integrations with other apps
- Small community compared to Notion or ClickUp

**Pricing:** Free with in-app purchases; Premium ~$4/month

---

### 6. Monday.com — Best for Team Goal Management

**Best for:** Enterprise teams tracking departmental goals alongside projects

Monday.com's Goals feature lets you create company-level OKRs, track progress with visual dashboards, and connect strategic goals to daily work items.

**Strengths:**
- Strong visual dashboards
- Connects goals to work items
- Executive reporting views
- 200+ integrations

**Limitations:**
- Expensive at scale
- More project management than personal goals
- Learning curve for new users

**Pricing:** From $9/user/month; Goals feature on Standard and above

---

### 7. Habitica — Best Gamified Goal Tracker

**Best for:** People motivated by game-like rewards and social accountability

Habitica turns your goals and habits into an RPG game. Complete real-world tasks to level up your character, earn gear, and join parties with friends for group accountability challenges.

**Strengths:**
- Unique gamification layer
- Strong community and social features
- Supports habits, dailies, and to-dos
- Free plan is fully functional

**Limitations:**
- Visual style isn't for everyone
- Can feel childish in professional contexts
- Gamification can become distracting

**Pricing:** Free; Subscriber $9/month

## How to Choose the Right Goal App

**For individuals:** Start with Strides (iOS) or Goalify. If you want more customization, build a system in Notion using a free goal-tracking template.

**For small teams:** ClickUp Goals offers the best value — goals connect directly to tasks without a separate tool.

**For enterprise OKRs:** Weekdone or Monday.com are purpose-built for organizational goal alignment.

**For motivation problems:** Habitica's gamification is genuinely effective for people who struggle with follow-through.

## Tips for Actually Achieving Your Goals

1. **Limit to 3-5 active goals** — spreading attention kills progress
2. **Set weekly milestones** — break annual goals into 12-week targets
3. **Schedule weekly reviews** — every Sunday, check progress and plan next week
4. **Make goals specific and measurable** — "run 3x per week" beats "get fit"
5. **Use implementation intentions** — "I will do X at Y time in Z place"

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best free goal setting app?

Notion and Habitica both offer fully functional free plans. Notion is better for structured, custom frameworks; Habitica is better if you need motivation and gamification.

### Is Notion good for goal setting?

Yes — Notion is excellent for goal setting if you invest time building or using a template. Its linked databases allow you to connect goals to projects, weekly reviews, and daily tasks in a single workspace.

### What app do high performers use for goals?

Many high performers use a combination: Notion or Obsidian for their goal system, ClickUp or Linear for task execution, and a physical journal for weekly reviews. The specific app matters less than having a consistent review ritual.

### How often should I review my goals?

Daily: check active habits/tasks. Weekly: review progress on short-term goals. Monthly: assess milestones. Quarterly: full goal review and adjustment. Annual: set new year goals and archive completed ones.
