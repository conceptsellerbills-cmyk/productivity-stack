# Best App Blockers & Focus Tools in 2025: Block Distractions, Get More Done

The average knowledge worker checks their phone 96 times per day. App blockers and focus tools create digital barriers that protect your attention during deep work sessions. Monthly search volume: ~18,000/month.

## Why Use an App Blocker?

Willpower is finite. App blockers remove the decision entirely — when a site is blocked, there's no friction-free path to distraction. Studies show that even brief interruptions (2-3 seconds) can derail a focused work state for up to 23 minutes.

## Top 7 App Blockers and Focus Tools for 2025

### 1. Freedom — Best Cross-Platform App Blocker

**Best for:** Professionals who need blocking across all devices simultaneously

Freedom blocks distracting websites and apps across Mac, Windows, iOS, Android, and Chrome — all in sync. A single "session" blocks the same sites everywhere, making it the most comprehensive option for multi-device users.

**Strengths:**
- Blocks websites AND apps (not just browsers)
- Syncs sessions across all devices simultaneously
- Locked mode prevents override until timer ends
- Recurring session scheduling
- 7 million+ users

**Limitations:**
- No free plan (only 7 free sessions at signup)
- Some users find setup initially complex
- No Pomodoro timer built-in

**Pricing:** $3.33/month (annual); $6.99/month (monthly); $129 lifetime

---

### 2. Cold Turkey — Most Aggressive Blocker for Windows/Mac

**Best for:** People who need an unbreakable blocker that can't be bypassed

Cold Turkey earns its name — once a block starts, there is no way to override it until the timer ends. It can even lock you out of your entire computer except approved apps during a focus session.

**Strengths:**
- Truly unbreakable blocks (can't be bypassed)
- Can block the entire computer except a whitelist
- Block by schedule, timer, or task completion
- Frozen Turkey mode locks everything
- One-time purchase available

**Limitations:**
- Desktop only (Windows/Mac — no mobile)
- Aggressive UX can frustrate users in genuine emergencies
- Free version limited to 1 block list

**Pricing:** Free (1 block list); Cold Turkey Blocker Pro $39 one-time

### 3. RescueTime — Best for Passive Time Tracking + Blocking

**Best for:** Data-driven people who want to understand their distraction patterns before blocking

RescueTime runs silently in the background, automatically tracking time spent in every app and website. Its Focus Sessions feature then blocks distractions on demand. The combination of awareness data and blocking is uniquely powerful.

**Strengths:**
- Automatic time tracking across all apps
- FocusTime blocks distractions with one click
- Weekly productivity reports and trends
- [Goal setting](/best-goal-setting-apps-2025) with daily focus time targets
- Works on Mac, Windows, Android (iOS limited)

**Limitations:**
- Expensive compared to pure blockers
- iOS tracking very limited (Apple restrictions)
- Privacy concern: tracks everything by default

**Pricing:** Free (basic tracking); RescueTime Premium $12/month or $78/year

### 4. SelfControl (Mac) — Best Free Mac App Blocker

**Best for:** Mac users who want a simple, free, and unbreakable blocker

SelfControl is a free, open-source Mac app that blocks websites for a set time period — and cannot be undone even by restarting your computer or deleting the app. Simple and brutally effective.

**Strengths:**
- Completely free
- Cannot be bypassed (even by reinstall or restart)
- Blacklist or whitelist mode
- No account or signup required

**Limitations:**
- Mac only
- No scheduling (manual only)
- Very basic UI — no stats or reports
- No app blocking, only websites

**Pricing:** Free (open-source)

**Best for:** People motivated by visual progress and environmental meaning

Forest gamifies focus sessions — plant a virtual tree when you start working, and it grows as long as you stay focused. Leave the app and your tree dies. Users can also spend earned coins to plant real trees through the company's reforestation partner.

**Strengths:**
- Charming visual motivation system
- Real tree planting with earned coins
- Social challenge features
- Available on iOS, Android, and Chrome extension

**Limitations:**
- Blocking only works within the Forest app (mobile)
- Chrome extension blocks sites but not apps
- Not as powerful as Freedom or Cold Turkey

**Pricing:** $1.99 (iOS/Android); Free Chrome extension with premium features

Opal is the premier iPhone app blocker, integrating deeply with iOS Screen Time to block apps at the OS level. Its Focus Profiles let you create different blocking configurations for work, deep focus, and personal time.

**Strengths:**

### 7. LeechBlock NG — Best Free Browser Extension

**Best for:** Budget-conscious users who mostly need to block time-wasting websites in their browser

LeechBlock NG is a free Firefox/Chrome extension that blocks specified websites during set time windows. It's not as powerful as Freedom but covers the most common distraction scenario — falling into a browser rabbit hole.

**Strengths:**
- Completely free
- Flexible scheduling (specific days, hours, or time limits)
- Multiple block sets for different scenarios
- Lockdown mode prevents changes

**Limitations:**
- Browser extension only — no app blocking
- Requires setup for effective use
- Can be disabled by switching browsers

**Pricing:** Free

## FAQ

### Can you bypass app blockers?

Most can be bypassed with effort (reinstalling, VPNs, other devices). Cold Turkey on desktop and SelfControl on Mac are considered truly unbreakable — they modify hosts files and system settings that persist through reinstalls and restarts.

### Does blocking apps actually improve productivity?

Yes — research on "nudge theory" shows that removing frictionless access to distractions reduces usage by 20-60% even among motivated people. The key is setting blocks before you feel the urge, not after.

### What's the best free app blocker?

SelfControl (Mac) or LeechBlock (browser) for free, uncompromising options. Freedom offers 7 free sessions to try before purchasing.
