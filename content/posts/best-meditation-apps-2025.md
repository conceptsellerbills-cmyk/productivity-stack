# Best Meditation Apps 2025: Calm, Headspace & Top Alternatives

Meditation has moved from fringe practice to mainstream productivity tool. Used by executives, athletes and high performers worldwide, regular meditation practice improves focus, reduces stress and enhances emotional regulation — all of which directly support productivity and performance.

The challenge: building a consistent meditation habit requires guidance, variety and the right app to support your specific goals.

---

## Types of Meditation Apps

- **Guided meditation**: a narrator walks you through sessions (best for beginners)
- **Breathwork**: focuses on specific breathing patterns for different outcomes
- **Sleep**: sleep stories, soundscapes and wind-down routines
- **Unguided/timer**: a bell or chime to mark meditation sessions (for experienced practitioners)
- **Mindfulness training**: structured courses in mindfulness-based stress reduction

## Building a Meditation Practice: Getting Started

**Start with 5 minutes.** Research shows even 5 minutes of daily meditation produces measurable effects on stress and focus. Don't try to meditate 30 minutes on day one.

**Same time, same place.** Link your meditation to an existing anchor (morning coffee, after waking up, before bed). Consistency beats duration.

**Use guided meditation for 30 days.** Even if you eventually prefer unguided sitting, guided sessions teach the mechanics of attention — what to do when your mind wanders.

**Don't chase the "blank mind" myth.** Meditation is not about having no thoughts. It's about noticing when your mind wanders and returning attention to the breath. The noticing IS the practice.

## FAQ

**Which is better: Calm or Headspace?**
Headspace for structure and learning meditation technique. Calm for sleep content and daily variety. Both are excellent. Headspace's beginner course is arguably the best structured introduction to meditation available in an app.

**Is there a free alternative to Calm and Headspace?**
Insight Timer offers 70,000+ free sessions — more content than any paid app. Oak is the best free simple timer for iPhone. Both are excellent free alternatives.

**How long until I notice meditation benefits?**
Research suggests 8 weeks of consistent daily practice (even 10 minutes) produces measurable changes in brain structure. Most practitioners notice mood and focus improvements within 2–4 weeks.
